🧹 Clear Service Worker

If you're seeing service worker errors, click the button below to completely remove all service workers and clear the cache.

Manual Steps (if button doesn't work):

  1. Open DevTools (F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I)
  2. Go to Application tab (Chrome) or Storage tab (Firefox)
  3. Click Service Workers in the left sidebar
  4. Click Unregister for each service worker
  5. Click Clear site data button
  6. Close DevTools and refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R
